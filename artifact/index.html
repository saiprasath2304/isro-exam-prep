NEGATIVE MARKING

The 40% rule

Each wrong answer costs −0.25; a skip costs 0. Expected value of a guess:

  • Eliminate to 2 options → 50% guess odds → EV = +0.375. Always attempt.
  • Eliminate to 3 options → 33% odds → EV = +0.083. Still attempt.
  • Zero elimination, pure blind guess (25%) → EV = 0. Skip it — no gain, and it burns time.

Rule of thumb: if you can rule out even one option, guessing beats skipping.

One card per section — the recurring patterns

🅰️ General English

  • Spelling: say it syllable-by-syllable in your head — most traps are one doubled/missing letter (commendable, not commendible).
  • Synonym/Antonym: learn the word in a sentence, not isolation — you'll recall tone (formal/negative) faster than definitions.
  • Idioms: picture the literal scene ('wet behind the ears' = a newborn) — it almost always gives the figurative meaning away.
  • Sentence correction: check subject-verb agreement first, then preposition, then tense — in that order.
  • Para-jumbles: find the sentence that introduces the topic (no pronoun referring backward) — that's always first.

🅱️ Quantitative Aptitude

  • % change chains (successive discounts/profit): use x+y−xy/100, never add % directly.
  • Pipes/boats: convert everything to "rate per unit time" and add/subtract rates — never add times.
  • Ratio-based partnership: profit share ∝ (capital × time invested), always.
  • Number series/pattern (a□b=c): test simple ops first — sum, product, sum of squares, (a+b)(a−b) — in that order.
  • Geometry: know 3-4-5 / 8-15-17 / 6-8-10 right-triangle triples cold — they appear disguised constantly.

🅲 Reasoning

  • Coding-decoding: always test position-shift, then letter-value sum, then position-swap — in that order.
  • Blood relations/seating: draw it. Every time. Circle for circular seating, line with arrows for direction puzzles.
  • Mirror vs water image: mirror flips left-right (reverses order); water image flips top-bottom (time formula: 5:60 − given time).
  • Venn diagrams: ask "can one thing belong to both categories in real life?" — if yes, circles must overlap.
  • Direction puzzles: plot on an x-y grid with compass fixed (North=+y) — never trust mental rotation alone.

🅳 General Knowledge

  • Static GK (Constitution, geography, science) rewards structured lists — learn the ranking/sequence, not the isolated fact.
  • Current affairs (2022–23 window): ISRO missions, Quad/G20, award winners, appointments — skim news-year roundups.
  • Space-focused questions are a given for ICRB — know every recent ISRO mission name, rover, and vehicle cold.
  • When unsure, eliminate the option that's a real thing but wrong category (e.g. a company instead of a person) — cuts odds fast.
